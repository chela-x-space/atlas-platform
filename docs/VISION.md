# ATLAS Vision

## Mission

Help people understand the most important events happening in the world through verified, attributable, and organized intelligence.

## Vision

ATLAS becomes a trusted global intelligence platform that continuously collects evidence from authoritative providers, connects events and entities across domains, and presents what matters with clarity.

## Product position

ATLAS is a **Global Intelligence Platform**. It is not a generic news site, advertising surface, marketplace homepage, or AI-generated fact source.

## Target users

- Analysts and researchers
- Risk, security, and operations teams
- Organizations monitoring global developments
- Developers building evidence-based intelligence applications
- Informed decision-makers who require source provenance

## Non-goals

- Replacing originating authorities or emergency services
- Publishing fabricated, anonymous, or unattributed claims
- Using opaque AI scores as canonical intelligence
- Providing financial, medical, legal, or security advice
- Mixing digital-product promotion with intelligence prioritization
- Maximizing source count at the expense of trust

## Core philosophy

Evidence first. Deterministic before intelligent. Trust before scale.

ATLAS preserves canonical identity and attribution, makes uncertainty and unavailable data explicit, and introduces new capabilities only after their governance boundaries are understood.
